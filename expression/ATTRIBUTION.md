# Attribution: cell-type descriptions

`cell_types.jsonl` lists the 1813 cell-type and tissue descriptions the expression model accepts, in the exact format it was trained on. They are built from public metadata of three sources, credited below.

## ENCODE (855 descriptions)

Metadata from the ENCODE portal (https://www.encodeproject.org), freely available under its data use policy. Each ENCODE entry's `accession` is the ENCODE file accession it was built from.

> ENCODE Project Consortium et al. Expanded encyclopaedias of DNA elements in the human and mouse genomes. Nature 583, 699-710 (2020). https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-020-2493-4
>
> Luo Y. et al. New developments on the Encyclopedia of DNA Elements (ENCODE) data portal. Nucleic Acids Research 48, D882-D889 (2020). https://doi.org/10.1093/nar/gkz1062

## GTEx (110 descriptions)

Tissue metadata from the public GTEx v8 release (https://gtexportal.org). The Genotype-Tissue Expression (GTEx) Project was supported by the Common Fund of the Office of the Director of the National Institutes of Health, and by NCI, NHGRI, NHLBI, NIDA, NIMH, and NINDS.

> GTEx Consortium. The GTEx Consortium atlas of genetic regulatory effects across human tissues. Science 369, 1318-1330 (2020). https://doi.org/10.1126/science.aaz1776
